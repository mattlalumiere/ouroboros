Ouroboros packer

Pick files and folders, set a passphrase, and get one executable that unpacks itself.

1. What to pack

Tick the files and folders you want. Folders keep their full structure.

2. Passphrase

The passphrase cannot be recovered. If you lose it, the data is gone.
If set, the keyfile is needed as well as the passphrase when unpacking.
